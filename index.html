About Us

Chromelite Technologies Limited is a Kampala-based company specialising in branding, signage and structural steel supply. We help businesses, institutions and individuals build a strong visual presence and put up durable structures that stand the test of time.

Our team combines creative design with solid engineering know-how. We take pride in quality materials, careful workmanship and on-time delivery, and we work closely with every client from the first idea to the finished product.

Our Services

We offer a complete range of branding and fabrication services under one roof. Whether you need a shop sign that attracts customers or steel work that supports a building, we handle the job from design to installation.

Every service is tailored to your needs, budget and timeline. Explore our core offerings below, or contact us to discuss a custom order.

signage

Eye-catching indoor and outdoor signs, from shopfronts to billboards, designed to make your business noticed.

Vehicle Branding

Durable wraps and decals that turn your cars, trucks and fleet into moving advertisements.

Printing

Sharp, high-quality printing for banners, stickers, posters, flyers and promotional materials.

Steel Fabrication

Strong, precisely cut and welded steel structures, built to your specification and delivered on time.

Our Projects

Over the years, we have delivered branding and steel projects for clients across different industries. These include illuminated shopfront signs, full fleet branding for transport and logistics companies, and steel frames for commercial buildings.

Each project reflects our commitment to quality and attention to detail. From small business signs to larger structural works, we treat every job as a chance to show what dependable craftsmanship looks like.
